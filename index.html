🧼 Hand Hygiene

Hand hygiene helps prevent the spread of germs and infections. It protects patients, healthcare workers, and the community.

5 MOMENTS Of HAND HYGIENE
  1. Before touching a patient.
  2. Before a clean/aseptic procedure.
  3. After body fluid exposure risk.
  4. After touching a patient.
  5. After touching the patient's surroundings.

Choose the method

  • Use alcohol-based hand rub when hands are not visibly soiled and according to facility policy.
  • Use soap and water when hands are visibly soiled and in situations specified by your facility policy.
  • Keep nails and hands appropriate for clinical practice according to local policy.
Remember: Gloves do not replace hand hygiene.

😷 PPE Guide

Select PPE based on the anticipated exposure and the required precautions.

Before entering the patient area

  1. Assess the task and expected exposure.
  2. Check the required isolation/precaution category.
  3. Perform hand hygiene.
  4. Select the appropriate PPE.
  5. Put on PPE in the sequence required by your facility SOP.

Before removing PPE

  • Identify contaminated surfaces.
  • Avoid touching your face.
  • Remove PPE using the approved sequence.
  • Perform hand hygiene at the required points.
Important: Respiratory protection and other PPE requirements depend on the exposure risk and local isolation policy.

🛡️ Isolation Precautions

Follow your facility's current isolation signage and SOP.

When isolation is suspected/required

  1. Recognise the suspected infection or transmission risk.
  2. Apply the required precautions as soon as indicated.
  3. Use appropriate patient placement and signage.
  4. Use PPE according to the precaution and task.
  5. Communicate the precautions during handover and transfer.

Transport

  • Confirm whether transport is clinically necessary.
  • Inform the receiving area about required precautions.
  • Apply appropriate measures during transport.

🚨 Needlestick / Exposure

For occupational exposure to blood or body fluids. Follow your facility's exposure protocol.

IMMEDIATE ACTION
  1. Stop the task safely.
  2. For a sharps injury, wash the affected area promptly with soap and water. Do not aggressively scrub or squeeze the wound.
  3. For mucous-membrane exposure, flush promptly with water or according to the facility protocol.
  4. Report the exposure immediately to the designated supervisor/IPC/occupational health pathway.
  5. Complete the required incident documentation and follow the clinical assessment pathway.
Do not delay reporting. Exposure assessment and any indicated post-exposure management are time-sensitive. Follow the official facility pathway.

🧹 Cleaning & Disinfection

Use only approved products, concentrations and contact times.

Routine cleaning

  1. Perform hand hygiene and wear PPE appropriate to the task.
  2. Prepare the approved cleaning/disinfectant product as instructed.
  3. Clean from cleaner areas toward dirtier areas where applicable.
  4. Respect the manufacturer's/facility's required contact time.
  5. Dispose of waste safely and perform hand hygiene.

Blood/body fluid spill

  1. Restrict access to the affected area.
  2. Wear appropriate PPE.
  3. Remove visible contamination safely using the approved procedure.
  4. Apply the approved disinfectant according to the facility protocol.
  5. Dispose of materials as required and perform hand hygiene.
Important: Do not guess disinfectant concentration or contact time. Use your facility-approved product instructions.
